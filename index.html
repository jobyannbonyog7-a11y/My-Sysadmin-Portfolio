Roles & Responsibilities

The critical duties that keep enterprise systems alive and secure.

Install & Maintain OS

Deploy, configure, and maintain operating systems (Windows Server, Linux distributions) across physical and virtual environments ensuring stability and security.

User Accounts & Permissions

Manage Active Directory / LDAP users, groups, and access rights. Enforce least-privilege principles and handle identity lifecycle.

Monitor Performance

Continuously track CPU, memory, disk, and network metrics. Set up alerts and dashboards to detect bottlenecks before they impact users.

Backup & Recovery

Design and execute reliable backup strategies, test restores regularly, and maintain disaster recovery plans to protect critical data.

Troubleshoot Issues

Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, and network problems quickly. Use logs, diagnostic tools, and systematic methods to restore service.

Security & Hardening

Apply patches, configure firewalls, implement security baselines, and monitor for threats to keep infrastructure secure.
